Privacy Policy

SalaryWise, developed by suryanshu15 — last updated 13 October 2026

This policy explains what data SalaryWise collects, why, and who it's shared with. SalaryWise is a personal finance app: it helps you track salary, expenses, EMIs, and investments, and offers an optional AI chat feature ("Money Coach").

Data we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name and email addressTo create and manage your account (sign-in via Firebase Authentication)
Salary, rent, EMI, expenses, SIP, and tax figures you enterTo power the app's budgeting, scoring, and planning features
Purchase history (Pro unlock order ID, amount, timestamp)To process payment and unlock Pro features
Money Coach chat messages and the financial context you've enteredTo generate a response, only when you use the Money Coach chat feature

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or browsing history.

Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions) — hosts your account and app data, acting on our behalf as our infrastructure provider.
  • Razorpay — processes Pro-unlock payments. Purchase details (amount, order/payment ID) are shared with Razorpay to complete the transaction. We never see or store your full card, UPI, or banking credentials.
  • Google's Gemini API — if you use the Money Coach chat feature, your message and the financial figures you've entered are sent to Gemini to generate a reply. This only happens when you actively use that feature.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data security

Your financial figures, expense log, and Money Coach chat history are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, using a key protected by your password. Only encrypted data is stored in Firestore, so neither we nor our infrastructure provider can read it. Your Pro status and whether you've finished setup are stored unencrypted so the app can work. Data is also encrypted in transit (HTTPS) between the app, our Cloud Functions, and the third parties above.

Earlier versions of SalaryWise stored your financial figures and expense log without this encryption. As of 13 October 2026 we have deleted all of that unencrypted data from our servers. Your account, Pro purchase, and payment records were kept. If your data was affected, you'll be asked to set up your profile again the next time you log in, and from then on it is stored encrypted. Copies of the deleted data may remain in our database provider's backups for a limited period until they expire automatically.

If you reset a forgotten password, a device you've already used SalaryWise on keeps a copy of your key in its secure storage and uses it to re-protect your data with the new password the next time you log in. If you reset your password and then log in only on a new device, your previously saved data can't be unlocked and will be cleared. Your Pro purchase is not affected.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and its associated data (profile, financial inputs, Pro status) at any time from Account → Delete account in the app, or by emailing us — see our account deletion page for details. Payment records are retained after account deletion for financial and legal record-keeping requirements. Deleted data may persist in database backups for a limited period before it expires.

Children's privacy

SalaryWise is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the version published at this URL.
