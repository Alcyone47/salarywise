Example chat

I have ₹2 L saved. Should I prepay my bike loan or keep it as an emergency fund?

Your loan EMI is ₹9,000, about 11% of take-home, which is manageable. Your rent, EMI and living costs come to ₹55,000 a month, so a three-month cushion is ₹1.65 L. Keep that aside first, then put the remaining ₹35,000 toward the loan.

And after that?

Move the EMI you free up into your SIP. Going from ₹10,000 to ₹17,000 would take your investing score from 18 to 30 out of 30.
